Quantum tunneling calculator

A particle with energy E meets a rectangular barrier of height V₀ and width d. How likely is it to appear on the other side?

Inputs

All inputs are SI: joules, kilograms, metres. Scientific notation works (e.g. 9.11e-31). Width is needed because tunneling depends on how thick the barrier is.
